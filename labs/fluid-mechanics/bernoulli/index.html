Bernoulli Principle.
Rebuilt from first principles.

A completely new educational experience for observing energy conservation, questioning assumptions and verifying the extended Bernoulli equation term by term.

A new laboratory architecture.

This concept introduces a new visual language, equation engine and teaching method built around scientific verification.

Physics to verify

The equation is the centre of the game

Instead of only displaying calculated values, the new lab will show where every energy term comes from and whether the selected assumptions remain valid.

Extended Bernoulli equation
p₁/ρg + α₁V₁²/2g + z₁ + Hₚ = p₂/ρg + α₂V₂²/2g + z₂ + hL
Pressure head + kinetic head + elevation head + pump head = downstream energy + linear and singular losses.
  • Expose both sides of the equation and the numerical residual.
  • Separate elevation, pressure, kinetic, pump and loss contributions.
  • Warn when steady, incompressible or one-dimensional assumptions are not appropriate.
  • Switch between ideal Bernoulli and the real extended energy equation.
