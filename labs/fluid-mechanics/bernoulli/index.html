CALCULATION METHOD

What the engine calculates

CONTINUITYQ = A₁V₁ = A₂V₂Velocity follows directly from the selected flow rate and diameters.
REYNOLDSRe = ρVD / μThe flow regime is evaluated independently in each section.
LINEAR LOSShf = f (L/D₂) V₂²/2gDarcy–Weisbach using section 2 as the effective loss section.
MINOR LOSShs = ΣK · V₂²/2gAll user-defined singular-loss coefficients are referenced to V₂.

Friction model: (f=64/Re) for laminar flow and the Swamee–Jain explicit correlation for turbulent flow; the transition range is interpolated for continuity.
